# Legal notes

*This is a description of the project, not legal advice.*

Outsider PS5 lets people play RPG Maker MV and MZ games **they own or are licensed to play, or that are free**, on a
PlayStation 5 they have already jailbroken. It contains no games, no exploit code, no Sony files, keys or firmware, and
nothing that finds, downloads or unlocks games. MV and MZ games store the key for their own encrypted images and sounds
in their `System.json`; the runtime uses it to read the files, as the official player does. Nothing is cracked.

The project does not help with copying or playing games without the right to. People who use it are responsible for
what they run. Rights holders who think something here infringes their rights: open an issue.

Trademarks (RPG Maker, PlayStation and others) belong to their owners; this project is not affiliated with them.
